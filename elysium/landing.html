What it is

  • HYPE pad: launch a token straight into a locked Uniswap V4 pool on Elysium. The whole supply is seeded at once and the liquidity can never be removed.
  • Hypeliysium Swap: an unmodified Uniswap V3 DEX on Elysium with standard 0.05% / 0.3% / 1% pools.
  • Bridge: the official Kinetiq bridge between HyperEVM and Elysium, called from your own wallet. We never hold your funds.
  • Platform token name and ticker are undecided. This page is not a token offering.

The platform cut on pad launches is a fixed 1% of trading volume, written into the contract (a 0.5% keeper bounty comes out of collected fees first). Creators set their own fee above that. Everything is shown in the app before you sign.

Network details
  • Elysium is an early-live network; its official public-mainnet status has not been verified.
  • Unaudited contracts, live with small caps. Only use money you can lose.
  • HYPE pad liquidity is locked forever by design: there is no admin, no pause and no upgrade on launched pools.
  • WHYPE (Kinetiq aeWETH) is upgradeable by Kinetiq. The Uniswap V4 PoolManager owner on Elysium is an external EOA that can set a protocol fee of up to 0.1%.
  • Holder rewards are fee-share payouts and may be treated as dividends where you live.
