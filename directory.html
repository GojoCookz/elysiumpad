How to use this without getting rekt
  1. Check the layer tag. HyperCore, HyperEVM, Elysium and Solana are different places with different risks.

Know the layers

HyperCore
Hyperliquid’s own order-book chain: perps, spot and staking.
HyperEVM
Hyperliquid’s smart-contract chain, sharing state with HyperCore.
Elysium
Kinetiq’s Hyperliquid L2. Public mainnet announced for Nov 9, 2026.
Solana
Runs on Solana. “HYPE” here may be a bridged Solana token, not native HYPE.
Multi
Spans several layers or chains.
