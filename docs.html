1. Put your idea on the stage.

Current live launch execution is Pump.fun on Solana. HYPE here is the registered Solana quote token—not a native Hyperliquid or Elysium deployment.

  1. Open Create. Connect Jupiter, Phantom or another compatible Solana wallet.
  2. Enter coin name, ticker, description and artwork. Select HYPE · Solana quote asset, then Review coin details.
  3. Keep Pump.fun selected. Read the live quote check and fee/risk disclosures. An unsupported quote cannot launch.
  4. Check consent to publish artwork and metadata. Click Upload metadata & review Pump launch. Published metadata may remain public even if you cancel.
  5. Inspect the mint, quote, creator fee and simulated SOL cost. Click Approve Pump launch in wallet, inspect the wallet request and approve only if correct.
  6. Use the transaction link to inspect confirmation. If pending, check the existing signature rather than submitting another launch.

Saving a draft does not deploy a token. No fixed SOL spend ceiling is imposed. You need sufficient SOL for actual fees and account rent; the review shows a simulation, not a guaranteed final charge.

2. Read the fees. Yes, all of them.

  • 3% native creator trading fee on buys and 3% on sells for supported Pump custom-quote launches, paid to the connected creator. Protocol/network fees are additional.
  • This is not a wallet-to-wallet transfer tax. It does not automatically fund holder rewards.
  • xHYPE, xBTC and xSOL are not currently admitted for this launch mode. SOL/USDC use a different fee schedule and cannot use this fixed-3% mode.
  • A quote paired with a leveraged token does not make your new coin itself leveraged.
  • Tokens can lose all value. Liquidity, backing, exit availability and returns are not guaranteed. A source listing or verified mint is not a security audit.

HypurrLabs does not ask for seed phrases or private keys. You review and sign wallet transactions yourself. Never paste keys into chat or a form.

3. Make a plan before opening the desk.

  • Mobile: Markets → select a sourced pair → Desk. Switch Chart, Trade and Positions. Rotate the phone for a wider layout; Full screen is optional where supported.
  • DEX Screener: the default chart embeds the provider’s own source-pool page. It is not a direct TradingView market-data API or a promise of every indicator. If blocked, open the provider or select Source candles.
  • Source candles: independent source history and local paper-trade overlays. Embedded provider charts cannot receive your wallet or paper overlays from this app.
  • Live trading remains paused. Connecting a wallet does not enable Review swap. Public creation and live swaps are separate switches.
  • Paper rehearsal: simulated funds and browser-local records only. It does not sign or submit a real swap.
  • Live holdings, simulated positions and historical P/L are different things. Without acquisition history, live cost basis remains unknown.

4. Bridging in is not proof you can get out.

Elysium documentation describes this withdrawal lifecycle:

  1. Initiate withdrawal on Elysium.
  2. Wait for the applicable challenge/finalization stage.
  3. Claim on HyperEVM. The Elysium transaction alone is not delivery to your destination wallet.

Reports of exit difficulty are not proof of a network-wide shutdown. We have not verified an end-to-end early-live mainnet exit or a universal challenge duration. Keep the withdrawal transaction, bridge URL, source chain and claim status when investigating a stuck transfer.
