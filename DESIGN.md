# Hypeliysium visual direction

## Market spacing and source hierarchy

The shared market rhythm uses 12 px card gaps and metric gutters, 16 px standard card padding, and 44 px minimum controls. Identity, metrics, source details and actions follow normal document flow; cards do not use spacer heights or automatic footer expansion. Dense layouts wrap names and values. Toolbar labels share an 18 px line box and 6 px control gap; view, grid and network actions are labeled groups.

Network status belongs beside the chain selector. The hero's two columns align at their top edges with deliberate internal padding. Sharp corners, existing theme tokens and aquatic motion remain the visual language. Verify at 1494×1002 and 390×844, with 3/6/9-column preferences and light, dark and blossom themes.

Elysium's primary metrics are USD only when verified; native numbers and rate provenance live in expandable details. Pair labels include the actual quote asset and Elysium. Cached and unavailable are distinct states.

Owner-pinned reference: Stockium's compact navigation, editorial hero, right-hand
token feature, dense market grid and two-column creation/trade workspaces.
Original expression: geometric H, deep petrol and sea-glass surfaces, open rail
geometry rather than copied illustrations. Real HLYS identity anchors the hero.

The revised opening uses up to 56px desktop / 42px mobile type, 1.04 leading and
a 1.4:1 split. Header and content share a 1280px frame with 24px desktop / 16px
mobile gutters. Controls use 44px targets; panels and controls use sharp edges
and thin tonal boundaries. Mobile stacks content in reading order. Existing
themes and language controls remain authoritative.

Two static SVG caustic textures provide slow, transform-only aquatic light behind the
hero. Motion pauses offscreen, in a hidden document, or through a persisted
manual toggle. Reduced-motion preference disables it. No animation implies live
market activity. Desktop density choices persist as 3, 6 or 9 columns; narrower
viewports reduce the rendered column count without overwriting the preference.
Mobile HLYS mint and explorer links live in a native disclosure to keep the
market entry reachable. Dense cards wrap symbols rather than truncating them.

Data remains sourced. Unavailable history is an explicit state, never a synthetic
chart. The optional game retains its separate frozen design and runtime.
